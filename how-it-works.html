Steps to Join an Expedition

  1. Pick a Trip: Browse upcoming expeditions and find one that fits your schedule, travel style, and comfort level.
  2. Apply or Ask Questions: Send a quick inquiry so Elda can ensure the trip is a good fit.
  3. Save Your Spot: Once approved, place your deposit and gain access to trip details.
  4. Show Up Ready: Receive packing tips and travel notes before departure.
