How it works

From the moment you spot a trip to the moment you land back home — here's exactly what to expect, in four straightforward steps.

01

Pick a trip

Browse the upcoming expeditions and find one that fits your schedule, travel style, and comfort level. Each trip page lists the dates, price, pace, group size, and exactly who the trip is designed for — so you can quickly tell if it's a fit before reaching out.

Not seeing the right one? Tell Elda what you're looking for and she'll let you know what's coming up next.

Browse all trips · 5 minutes
02

Apply or ask questions

Send a quick inquiry through the trip page. It's not an application in any formal sense — just a short note about you, what you're hoping for, and any questions. Elda reads every one personally and gets back within a couple of days.

If the trip's a good fit, she'll send next steps. If something else might suit you better, she'll say that too.

Reply within 48 hours · No pressure
03

Save your spot

Once you're approved, you'll place a deposit to lock in your seat (usually $300, applied to the trip cost). After that, you get access to the full trip details: rooming options, packing list, recommended flights, suggested arrival times, and a private group thread with the other travelers.

Final payment is due 60 days before departure. Payment plans are available on most trips — just ask.

$300 deposit · Final payment 60 days out
04

Show up ready

About two weeks out, you'll get a detailed pre-trip packet: weather, what to pack, what to expect each day, local customs worth knowing, and a few phrases in the local language. Elda's on call from that point on — questions, last-minute changes, anything.

The day you land, the trip starts. You don't think about logistics again until you're home.

Pre-trip packet · 24/7 support

Who handles what

Elda handles

  • Accommodation booking & vetting
  • All in-country transportation
  • Local guides & activity coordination
  • Restaurant reservations & group meals
  • Pace, rhythm, and a real itinerary
  • On-trip troubleshooting (24/7)
  • Group dynamics & intros

You handle

  • Your flights to and from the trip
  • Your passport & any visas
  • Travel insurance (required)
  • Personal expenses & tips
  • Showing up rested and ready
  • Saying yes to things you might not normally
Worth knowing

A trip, not a tour.

You In? trips are designed to feel like traveling with a small group of friends — just with someone behind the scenes who's already figured out the logistics, vetted the places, and built in time to actually enjoy them.

There's no clipboard, no group chant, no rushing to the next thing. Just a thoughtfully-paced week with the kind of detail you'd put in if it were your own trip.
