Trip overview

A week moving between Costa Rica's lush rainforest, mountain cloud forest, and laid-back Pacific coast.

Built for travelers who want a real introduction to the country — not a checklist of sights, but a chance to slow down, eat well, swim in waterfalls, learn a few Spanish phrases from the people who live there, and come home with stories you'll be telling for years.

This is a moderate-pace trip. Expect some walking and a couple of easy hikes, but also plenty of room to read on a balcony, nap in a hammock, or wander a town on your own.

Who this trip is for

Solo travelers, friend groups, couples, and first-time adventure travelers. Comfort level is mid-range — nice eco-lodges and a beachfront hotel, no roughing it. If you've never done a small-group trip before, this is a great first one.

Day-by-day itinerary

Day 01 · Thu

Arrive in San José

Pick-up from the airport and transfer to a quiet hotel in the city. Welcome dinner with the group at a local Costa Rican restaurant. No early start tomorrow — rest up.

Day 02 · Fri

San José → Arenal

Scenic drive to the Arenal volcano region (about 3 hours). Afternoon free at the lodge, then an evening soak in natural hot springs as the sun sets over the volcano.

Day 03 · Sat

Rainforest day

Guided hike across the Arenal hanging bridges through primary rainforest. Lunch at a local soda (family-run eatery). Free afternoon for kayaking, horseback riding, or a long lazy lunch.

Day 04 · Sun

Arenal → Monteverde

Travel to the cloud forest via boat-across-the-lake route. Late-afternoon arrival in Monteverde, group dinner with views over the misty mountains.

Day 05 · Mon

Cloud forest morning, head to the coast

Early walk through the Santa Elena cloud forest (quetzals, hummingbirds, dripping moss). Mid-day transfer down to the Pacific coast. Sunset toes-in-the-sand welcome.

Day 06 · Tue

Coast & snorkel

Boat trip to a nearby island for snorkeling, beach time, and a fresh seafood lunch. Free afternoon — surf lesson, spa, or hammock. Group dinner on the beach.

Day 07 · Wed

Village visit & cooking class

Visit a small inland village for a traditional cooking class with a local family. You'll come back with a recipe or two. Farewell dinner that night.

Day 08 · Thu

Depart

Transfer to San José airport for your flight home. (Stay an extra night or two if you'd like — Elda can help arrange it.)

Two nights at an eco-lodge in the Arenal volcano region, two nights at a boutique cloud-forest inn near Monteverde, and three nights at a small beachfront hotel on the Pacific coast. All rooms are private with en-suite bathrooms. Solo travelers can choose a private room (no single supplement) or opt to share.
