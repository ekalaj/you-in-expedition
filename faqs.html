Common Questions

Do I need to travel with someone?

No. Many travelers come solo. The trips are built to feel welcoming from the start.

Are flights included?

Usually, flights are not included unless stated on the trip page.

How many people are on each trip?

Most expeditions are small-group trips, usually around 6–12 people.

Do I need experience?

It depends on the trip. Each expedition page will list the comfort level, activity level, and what to expect.

Can I pay a deposit?

Yes. Each trip can offer a deposit option, payment schedule, and final payment deadline.
