Frequently asked questions

Most travelers have the same handful of questions before their first trip — here are the answers. If your question isn't covered, send Elda a note.

About the trips

How many people are on each trip?

Small groups, usually 6–12 travelers. Big enough that you'll always have someone to share a meal or activity with. Small enough that the trip feels personal, not packaged.

Do I need to travel with someone?

Nope. Many travelers come solo — the trips are designed that way from the start. There's a welcome dinner on the first night so everyone meets quickly, and the pace lets you join in or take quiet time as you want.

What's the typical age range?

It varies, but most travelers are between their late twenties and early sixties. Trips skew toward thoughtful, curious people — not party trips, not retiree tours. Each expedition page lists the typical mix so you know what to expect.

Do I need experience or be in shape?

Depends on the trip. Each expedition page lists the comfort level, activity level, and what to expect day by day. Most trips are designed for first-time adventure travelers with normal fitness — nothing technical. If something needs more experience, it'll say so plainly.

Booking & payment

How do I book?

Submit a short inquiry from the trip page. Elda will reply within a couple of days. If the trip's a good fit, she'll send instructions to place your deposit and lock your spot.

Can I pay a deposit?

Yes. Every trip has a deposit option (usually $300) that holds your spot. The remaining balance is due 60 days before departure. Payment plans are available on most trips — just ask.

What's the cancellation policy?

Deposits are non-refundable but transferable to another trip within 12 months. Cancellations more than 60 days out: full refund minus deposit. Inside 60 days: depends on what can be recovered from vendors. Travel insurance is required for this reason.

Are there hidden costs?

No. Each trip page lists exactly what's included and what's not. The price you see is what you pay — aside from flights, insurance, and personal spending money.

Flights & logistics

Are flights included?

Usually not, unless stated on the trip page. You'll book your own international flights so you have flexibility on dates, departure city, and frequent flyer programs. Recommended arrival/departure windows are listed on each trip page.

Do I need a visa?

It depends on your passport and destination. Visa requirements are listed on each trip page — and Elda will flag anything specific in your welcome packet. Most travelers from the US, UK, EU, and Canada don't need advance visas for any current trips.

Is travel insurance required?

Yes — it's required for every trip. It protects you in case of cancellation, medical issues, or trip disruption. Elda will share recommended providers when you book.

What if my flight gets delayed or canceled?

Let Elda know as soon as you can. She'll adjust your transfer and let the group know. It happens — we plan for it.

On the trip

Will I have free time?

Yes. Every trip builds in unscheduled time — afternoons to nap, wander, journal, or join an optional activity. The point is to feel like a real week, not a marathon.

What kind of accommodation will we stay in?

Eco-lodges, small boutique hotels, beachfront inns, riads, ryokans — whatever fits the destination. All vetted personally. Always private rooms with en-suite bathrooms.

What about dietary restrictions?

Tell Elda when you book. Vegetarian, gluten-free, allergies, religious requirements — all easy to accommodate. Just give as much notice as you can.

Will there be Wi-Fi?

At all accommodations, yes. Out in the field (hikes, boats, remote villages), often not. Most travelers find the disconnection is part of what makes the trip feel like a trip.

Still didn't see your question?

Drop Elda a note — she'll get back to you personally, usually within a couple of days.
